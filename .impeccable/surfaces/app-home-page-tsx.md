# Home (/home)

Mode: Persuade. Visitors: mostly a host about to create a group (often on mobile), plus logged-in returning users looking for their rooms. Primary action: create a group (working form in the first viewport). Secondary: join with an invite code, open a joined room, log in (optional).

Constraints: keep the board/tile/pawn world, pink accent, dark purple ground, headline copy and Korean voice. No invented stats or testimonials. Group page stays as is.

## Direction contract

THESIS: The home page is a game board, and the START tile is where you make a group. Refuses a centered headline over a stack of equal form cards.

OWN-WORLD: Calendar tiles with a bottom lip (cell, cell-edge, scheduled pink). Ink is limited to pink, lavender/purple, and tangerine. Empty tiles are designed ghost tiles with dashed edges, not gray filler. The logo is a heavy SVG Q, its tail a forward arrow, matching the Bowlby wordmark weight.

STORY: In one glance the visitor sees four steps on a stair-step path: make, share the link, friends join with a nickname, the date is fixed. They type a group name on START. The pawn hops up the stairs while the group is created, then the visitor lands on the calendar.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Top bar has the logo on the left and login on the right. A large left-aligned headline sits upper left. The stair path rises from a big START tile (bottom left, with the form) through 링크 공유 and 닉네임 참여 to the GOAL tile (upper right). The invite-code line sits under START. On mobile the path stacks vertically: START first, then a zigzag below it.

FORM: board path as hero, #1 of 7 on my list (dealt with #7 countdown and #3 game box). Raises: from the seven-segment world, empty cells drawn as deliberately as lit ones (ghost tiles). From the origami world, one continuous path, with no floating card stack. From the kiosk world, a strict three-ink set. seed:438458e0f9a61596
